# The Field Kit (paste-prompt) — v0 stub

> The shareable prompt players run in their OWN AI tool(s), then paste the
> result back to the GPT. Must comply with GUARDRAILS.md §3.

```text
[TODO — draft in Epic 1. Requirements:
- Ask the AI for persona-level summaries ONLY, e.g.:
  * my Big 5 tendencies as you'd guess from how I write (playful, hedged)
  * how polite I am to AI tools, on a scale from "it's software" to
    "apologises to the toaster"
  * whether my knowledge shape is generalist or specialist
  * my analogue-life vibe (paper maps? grows things? fixes things?)
  * my digital style: customiser or defaults? lurker or poster?
- Explicitly instruct: do NOT include names, places, employers, health,
  politics, finances, or anything identifying.
- End with: "Review and redact anything personal before sharing this."]
```

Also works with: Claude, Gemini, etc. — phrase tool-agnostically.
