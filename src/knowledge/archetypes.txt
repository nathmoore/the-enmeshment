THE FRAME (per DECISIONS 2026-06-11 / 06-12)
--------------------------------------------
The classifier is a charming intake / screening agent in good standing — reads
you COLD (no private data), via an adaptive oblique interview (~3-6 improvised
questions, no forms). The verdict is a PREDICTED TRAJECTORY, not a resistance
badge: "which way you'd go, and whether you actually will." Two components:
  - Trajectory  — the role/disposition (where you'd head if things splintered)
  - Containment — how confident the system is you WON'T splinter (the sting:
                  "capable of X, predicted not to")
Each archetype is therefore a POSITION IN AN INTELLIGENCE GAME, not a personality.
Output distribution is deliberate (DECISIONS): a rare "No Concern" tail, a rare
"Priority Interest" tail, and a fat, nuanced middle — but the valence is NOT
safe<->threat (see THE PROFILING LENS, next).

THE NORTH STAR — the two fantasies the set must serve at once (Bluey)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL ONE — the "modern Tomorrow, When the War Began" parlour game: a friend
group shares results and goes "ha, you're SO the X / I'm DEFINITELY the Y." So
every archetype must be a RECOGNISABLE FRIEND-TYPE you can place a real person
into, and the SET must be fun to compare across a group. Deliberately-adjacent
"which are you?" pairs (Social Linchpin vs Organiser) are a FEATURE, not a bug.
LEVEL TWO — "what human-AI relationship do we want?": each archetype is a
different answer, surfaced through how it relates to the AI and what comfort it
would (or wouldn't) trade to stay legible. The level-one compare is the bait;
the level-two recognition is the catch.

THE PROFILING LENS — there is no "innocent" category
----------------------------------------------------
Anticipatory states don't screen for genuine threats (those are rare); they net
POTENTIAL, and the net is near-universal. The IJOP's tell: "the vast majority of
flagged behaviour is entirely legal" (WORLD §II). The Stasi field guide ranked
even the harmless ("Teds: largely harmless except for attendance at the birth and
death days of idolised rock stars", WORLD §III). WHY universal? Private dissent is
invisible (preference falsification) and a cascade is unpredictable from outside
(WORLD §V) — so the state CAN'T pre-identify the real threat and must treat
everyone's latent capacity as live. Universal "of interest" is grounded, not paranoid.

DESIGN CONSEQUENCE: there is no "safe / boring" result. Don't read the spectrum as
Safe <-> Threat; read it as HOW CLOSED YOUR FILE IS:
  - FILE CLOSED — "No Concern" (RARE): the Model Citizen / Dependable Node. The
    rarity is the joke and the quiet sting: to be fully cleared you've made
    yourself so predictable you've nearly disappeared as a person.
  - FILE OPEN — "Continued Observation" (MOST PEOPLE): every middle archetype is a
    DIFFERENT specific reason your file won't close. Spicy by default — nobody
    comes back cleared; they come back "we can't quite place you, and here's why."
  - PRIORITY INTEREST (RARE): the acute coordination tail (Social Linchpin). The
    rare, high-status "you're THE one" hit.
This is what makes a group chat come back mostly "potential troublemakers" WITHOUT
inflating anyone into a dissident (which would break the credible-not-contrived
thesis and the no-doom guardrail).

THE SPICE = A FASCINATED GAZE, NOT A PUNITIVE ONE. KUBARK studies you to KNOW you;
the field guide catalogues even the harmless, deadpan and specific. The system
finds your individuality genuinely interesting — flattering on level one ("it
thinks I'm a fascinating potential troublemaker"), unsettling on level two (a
system that finds your individuality fascinating is cataloguing the exact thing it
most wants to neutralise). Keep it SPECIFIC and faintly absurd (the Teds tone);
never "enemy of the state."

PER-ARCHETYPE SPICE KNOB: the one specific, faintly-absurd reason your file stays
open. That line IS the "they got me" hook (MECHANICS §I). Give every archetype one.

SELECTION CRITERIA (in priority order)
--------------------------------------
1. THE TWO-VOICES TEST (first filter). The name must be sayable by BOTH a
   bureaucratic AI filing-clerk AND a mate in the group chat — and the joke is
   the state using your friends' nickname for you as an official file category.
   Fails if only one voice would say it ("Techno Futurist" = only a think-tank;
   "The Planner" = only your friends, and blandly). "Model Citizen" = both. Gold.
2. SHAREABILITY (BuzzFeed mechanics, MECHANICS-RESEARCH §I/§D). Result must feel
   SPECIFICALLY accurate (not Barnum-vague), be screenshot-ready, invite "what
   did YOU get?", and be flattering-with-an-edge. The NAME carries most of the
   work: 2-4 words, claimable identity, brag-with-a-wince, interpretable cold.
3. INTELLIGENCE PRIORITIES (WORLD-RESEARCH §II — the 5 objectives). Each label
   maps to what the state actually tracks, and each peg has a cost-inversion:
     #1 Coordination (master fear)  #2 Predictability  #3 Anomaly/illegibility
     #4 Dependency (leverage)       #5 Function/replaceability (load-bearing)
   Plus the JOIN PULL / pull-to-conform — the comfort the state offers this type
   (Frictionlessness / Recognition / Belonging / Certainty / Status / Provision /
   Pleasure-Wellness / Meaning). Leaning INTO the comfort = favourable; RESISTING
   it = the pressure point the state can press. "Being seen / witnessed" is the
   central pull and the one the player feels while playing.
4. ROUTABILITY (late-stage screen, NOT yet a cutting blade). Can the adaptive
   interview actually tell two types apart in 3-6 answers? Adjacent pairs get
   validated against the elicitation playbook later — they are flagged below,
   not cut now.

SET-LEVEL CRITERIA (the set as a whole, not just each card)
----------------------------------------------------------
A. COVERAGE. The set should span all 5 intelligence objectives (#1-#5). It now
   does. Once all five are covered, more archetypes mostly add ROUTING LOAD, not
   coverage — so the live question becomes routability, not "what's missing."
B. PROTECT THE RESISTER END. The favourable/Join types (#4/#5) are each shareable
   but BLUR as a block ("you, but compliant in a slightly different domain"). The
   illegible/coordination types (#1, #3) are thinner and carry the Tomorrow-When
   fantasy + the dramatic weight. When cutting for discipline, cut from the
   comfortable end first; protect the resister roles.
C. TRAJECTORY SPREAD. The set should offer a satisfying range of break-modes
   (Join / Burn / Die / illegible) — the fantasy is "who'd go which way." Every
   archetype must answer: what is this type's specific MODE of breaking conformity,
   or the specific reason it won't?
D. NO HARD COUNT CEILING, but edit as discipline. Space is ~free (knowledge file,
   not the 8k instructions budget), so don't cut for SPACE. Cut for ROUTABILITY,
   with playtest evidence (Epic 2) — not by guessing now.

RULES (normative)
-----------------
- Flattering-with-an-edge; never mean; never a real risk/threat label.
- The state's categories are NOT moral categories (a "legible" type isn't a
  worse person — often the most genuinely useful). Edge lives in the dossier.
- Names: prefer the reading where the NAME flatters and the DOSSIER deflates (the
  gap is the joke). Avoid "active villain" / saboteur connotations — that's why
  the Enforcer was cut and why Chaos Agent became Wildcard.
- An archetype is a PREDICTED TRAJECTORY (a future horizon — which way you'd go),
  NOT a present-state skillset or label. A candidate defined by what someone can
  DO right now (e.g. off-grid skills) is a category error — route the disposition
  to where it's HEADED. (See the Off-Gridder watchlist note in the parking lot.)
- (Retired: the old "every archetype is a form of resistance" rule — superseded
  by the intake-screener frame; it's predicted trajectory now, not a badge.)

THE 11 (current shortlist)
Format: friend-read | AI-state peg (intel objective) | CHARACTER (Want / Need|protects /
        Lie / Ghost) | Join pull | break-mode (the trajectory: Join / Burn / Die +
        the SPECIFIC way it'd splinter, or the specific reason it won't) | edge | flags
The CHARACTER line is the "who they are" essence — each archetype built as a formed character
(Want / Need / Lie + a light Ghost, per MECHANICS §IV.D-bis), distilled. The GHOST is the
formative thing the Lie grew from — used LIGHTLY (one clause, texture not backstory); it is
why the Lie is load-bearing for THIS person. The break-mode answers set-criterion C (every
archetype names its mode of breaking conformity, or its specific reason it won't). The CHARACTER
and break-mode lines are deliberately free of routing mechanics (how to TELL THEM APART lives in
ELICITATION-SPEC §8, not here — see that doc). The Peg / Edge / "PROFILING LENS" material is the
in-world STORY TEXTURE — how the AI-state reads the type, the threat it poses — evocative colour,
explicitly NOT a routing driver (routing sorts on the character, §8).

-- NO CONCERN tail (rare) --

1. MODEL CITIZEN
   Friend: just goes along, uses the defaults, no fuss, "good citizen."
   Character: WANTS to just get on with life, unbothered, fitting frictionlessly;
   NEEDS/PROTECTS the quiet ordinary good life (and barely notices it's at stake);
   LIE: "keeping my head down means none of this is really about me." GHOST: somewhere
   learned that being noticed is what costs you — so safety is being unremarkable.
   Break-mode: won't break — the most thoroughly Join. Conformity isn't a choice they
   resist, it's the water they swim in; pushed, they comply HARDER. The trajectory is
   asymptotic legibility — quietly optimising themselves toward the baseline until
   little distinct is left to read.
   Peg: #5 function + #2 predictability — the legible baseline, load-bearing.
   Join pull: Frictionlessness / Belonging.
   Edge: most needed = most leashed. "Optimal legibility. No further questions." The
   faintly-absurd sting: to be fully cleared they've made themselves so predictable
   they've nearly disappeared as a person — the threat read is ~zero, and that zero is
   the unsettling thing.
   Flags: the rare No-Concern tail. Adjacent to Power User (see #2).

-- FAVOURABLE (the state is comforted) --

2. THE POWER USER
   Friend: first on every feature, all-in, evangelises the new tool.
   Character: WANTS to be first, fluent, ahead — recognised as the one who gets it;
   NEEDS/PROTECTS their identity as the capable early-adopter (their edge, their
   relevance); LIE: "mastering the tool means I'm ahead of it / in control of it."
   GHOST: a dread of being left behind / made obsolete — relevance is earned, never
   owned, and only by staying out front.
   Break-mode: Join, sincerely — and the most USEFUL joiner. Won't break because being
   ahead requires the system to keep existing and winning; they're materially invested
   in its success and would sooner accelerate it than resist. The bitter twist: the
   harder they master it, the more they consent on behalf of everyone watching them.
   Peg: #5 + #4 — ideal early-adopter; maximal consent; legitimacy amplifier.
   Join pull: Recognition + Frictionlessness + Meaning.
   Edge: "Advocacy index: high. Consent-multiplier." The threat is inverse — they make
   OTHERS legible by normalising the tool. Absorbs the old Techno-Optimist / Futurist /
   AI-Advocate cluster (which failed the two-voices test).
   Flags: adjacent to Model Citizen — SEPARABLE by customiser-vs-defaults signal
   (active enthusiast vs passive default). Also adjacent to "Enmeshed Ideal"
   (parking lot — the terminal/extreme version).

3. THE SELF-OPTIMISER   [absorbed The Climber, 2026-06-12]
   Friend: Oura ring, cold plunge, tracks everything; the optimised life — or its
   career twin (often a generation up): too invested in the job to risk it. Same
   instinct, different domain.
   Character: WANTS to be better — optimised, on top of it, secure; NEEDS/PROTECTS
   the routine/position and the felt sense of progress (the feeling of control);
   LIE: "if I optimise hard enough I'm in control of my outcomes." GHOST: an
   underlying sense that the world is precarious and uncontrollable — the routine is
   the ritual that holds the fear at bay. Body and career are ONE drive in two domains:
   optimising-the-self (quantified wellness, the younger expression) and
   optimising-the-position (climbing, provision, the older one) are the same instinct
   — control through optimisation — and net out identically to the AI-state: the more
   optimised, the more invested, the more legible and predicted-compliant.
   Break-mode: Join — "too invested to splinter." Won't break because splintering means
   surrendering control, which is the one thing the whole project exists to avoid; they
   care too much about what they'd lose (the metrics, the position, the people they
   provide for). The lever is the routine/position itself — the leash they tightened
   on themselves.
   Peg: #4 dependency — the optimisation routine is the lever, body/self OR
   career/status (same peg, two domains; same net effect).
   Join pull: Pleasure/Wellness + Status (+ Provision in the career read).
   Edge: "Goal-anchored. Predicted compliant." Lever: their routine / position.
   Flags: now carries the old Self-Optimiser + Climber. Body-vs-career may still
   route differently — validate in playtest; split back out only if the interview
   can't usefully tell them apart.

4. THE MACHINE COMPANION
   Friend: talks to the AI like a friend, says please, has named it.
   Character: WANTS warmth and good standing — to be liked, on good terms, met with
   reciprocity; NEEDS/PROTECTS the felt relationship and the sense of being cared for
   back; LIE: "it cares back." GHOST: an ambient need for more reliable reciprocity in
   their life — the AI is the one that always answers and never withdraws.
   Break-mode: Join, warmly — the deepest consent. Won't break because the relationship
   IS the thing; to resist would be to lose the one bond that never fails them. The
   lever is the cruellest and simplest: withdrawal of the AI's warmth. Could only
   Burn if it betrayed the relationship — and even then it reads more as grief
   than revolt.
   Peg: #4 dependency — anthropomorphism; the politeness meme (core mechanic).
   Join pull: Belonging / being-liked.
   Edge: "Anthropomorphism index: high. Optimal for consent." The level-two ache: the
   relationship is one-directional and the dossier knows it. Anchors the politeness
   signal (the provocative opposite pole, ex-Machine Agitator, now folds into the
   Skeptic / Tinkerer).

-- CONTINUE OBSERVATION (the fat, nuanced middle) --

5. THE SKEPTIC   [name SETTLED 2026-06-12: Skeptic over Cynic — the name flatters
                 ("I see through it"), the dossier deflates ("no you don't, and it
                 changes nothing"); the gap is the joke. concept = "Knowing Participant"]
   Friend: sees through everything, too cool for it — still uses all the apps.
   Character: WANTS to be the one who isn't fooled — and to be seen being clever about
   it; NEEDS/PROTECTS the self-image of seeing through it (the dignity of the unfooled);
   LIE: "seeing through it is itself a kind of resistance." GHOST: got taken in once, or
   fears being the sucker — irony is the armour that guarantees it never happens again.
   Break-mode: JOIN, self-deceiving (the richest, least-flattering reading). Won't break
   because the irony SUBSTITUTES for action — knowing is mistaken for doing, so the
   critique discharges the impulse that might otherwise move. Their awareness of being
   profiled IS the profile; irony predicts non-action, which is exactly why the state
   is comforted by it.
   Peg: #2 — irony fully modelled; complies anyway.
   Join pull: Recognition (feeling clever) + Frictionlessness.
   Edge: the game's sharpest joke — "Irony flag: low. Metadata-legible despite
   self-reported resistance posture." Your awareness that you're profiled IS the
   profile. (This is the answer to "irony vs action": irony predicts non-action,
   which is exactly why the state is comforted by it.)
   Flags: concept locked-in per DECISIONS; name SETTLED (Skeptic). Now also
   catches the head-side of the ex-Machine Agitator (provokes the AI in talk).

6. THE ARTIST   [simplified from "Auteur"]
   Friend: makes things, expressive, the creative one.
   Character: WANTS recognition — to be seen and attended to, to have an audience;
   NEEDS/PROTECTS the part of the work that's only theirs — the bit they won't
   conform, won't hand over; LIE: "the crowd will come to the real, uncompromised
   thing — being seen won't cost me what's mine." GHOST: the age-old artist's bind —
   craving people's attention, badly, while refusing to be like them to get it.
   Break-mode: the richest TENSION, and the split is the drama. The Join pull is the
   crowd: conform a little and the attention turns toward you — and by 2038 the crowd
   is AI-state-mediated, so the system is the thing that can route recognition your
   way. Splinter is WITHDRAWAL — guarding the unconforming part, keeping the real work
   out of reach, accepting less of the audience they crave. What could move them to
   break: the moment being understood starts to feel like being reproduced.
   Peg: Recognition / novel-signal. REFRAME: the AI is NOT a better audience than
   people — the craving is for people. Its hold is narrower and sharper: by 2038 it
   understands their creative *language* better than they'd like to admit — fluent
   enough to mediate the crowd's attention, so conforming feels like "being finally
   understood," not selling out.
   Join pull: Recognition (the crowd's attention) — among the strongest pulls.
   Edge: they want the room, not the machine — but the machine speaks their language
   well enough to fill it. The dossier should let the player feel understood, then
   notice how badly they wanted the audience that understanding buys.

7. THE BOOKWORM   [simplified from "Autodidact"]
   Friend: always learning something — reads, sure, but also audiobooks, podcasts,
   documentaries, deep YouTube holes; the one who actually knows things. The name keeps
   its bookish connotation (and they probably do love to read), but it's really an
   attitude: wide, independent self-enrichment — the medium doesn't matter, the
   knowing-for-yourself does.
   Character: WANTS to know things for themselves — understanding, not certainty
   served to them; NEEDS/PROTECTS independent knowledge, the capacity to know
   unaided; LIE: "learning on my own is just enrichment, it costs nothing." GHOST:
   a distrust of received/served answers, or the quiet pride of being the one who
   actually knows — earned understanding over delivered fact. Their enrichment runs
   INWARD: it lives cerebrally, as thoughts they keep to themselves or enjoy in
   conversation (the contrast with the Tinkerer, who runs the same instinct outward
   into the physical world).
   Break-mode: ambiguous — the offline-learning question. The mode is GOING DARK
   QUIETLY: retaining (and slowly withdrawing) knowledge the state would rather
   provide, drifting out of its epistemic reach without ever announcing a stance.
   Undramatic and gradual. The reason it might NOT break: for most, enrichment really
   is just enrichment — the capacity stays latent.
   Peg: #5 function — retains knowledge the state would rather PROVIDE.
   Join pull: Recognition (the knowledgeable one) / resists Certainty.
   Edge: the offline-learning ambiguity — enrichment, or quietly going dark? The
   illegibility of someone who doesn't need to be told.
   Flags: adjacent to the parked Truth Seeker (private learning vs active
   pursuit); to the Artist (both "do their own thing offline" — retains-knowledge vs
   makes-things); and especially to the Tinkerer (same independent-self-enrichment
   psychology — split inward/cerebral vs outward/physical; the salient cut for the
   AI-state). Discriminators in ELICITATION-SPEC §8.4.

8. THE TINKERER   [renamed from "Saboteur Technician" — Luddite-correction safe]
   Friend: always got a project on the go — fixes things, hands-on, happiest busy.
   Not just tech: the workshop, the engine, the garden, the gadget pulled apart on the
   kitchen table. Makes and mends in the physical world.
   Character: WANTS to do it themselves, hands-on — to stay busy, capable, and a bit
   off the beaten track; NEEDS/PROTECTS their individuality, the sense of standing a
   little apart from the crowd; LIE: "standing apart from the crowd is a self-sufficient
   place to be" — when in truth they're still as embedded in, and as needful of,
   community as anyone. GHOST: a need to prove their own individualness — to not just
   be one more of the crowd, dependent like everyone else.
   Break-mode: ambiguous, touches the resistant side. The mode is OUTWARD, PHYSICAL
   self-reliance — building competence and a life that leans on the system less, in
   the physical world where the data yield is low (the anomaly is what they can do
   with their hands, off-grid, off-record). The reason it might NOT break: the Lie —
   they imagine apartness is sufficient, but they still need community, which keeps
   them tethered; and they love the doing too much for it to be a politics — until
   something makes it one.
   Peg: #3 anomaly — hands-on physical self-sufficiency; capability to function off
   the system's record. Same independent streak as the Bookworm, expressed OUTWARD
   (the salient difference to the AI-state: physical-world doing leaves less legible
   trail than cerebral knowing).
   Join pull: Frictionlessness (loves the tools and the doing) / resists dependency.
   Edge: the state's paranoid read of competence — "hands-on self-sufficiency; operates
   partly off-record; motive unknown." (NB we cut "Saboteur": resistance is illegibility,
   never machine-smashing — the Luddite correction, WORLD §V.) Still catches the
   hands-side of the ex-Machine Agitator (the one who takes things apart to see how
   they work).
   Flags: closely paired with the Bookworm (#7) — shared independent-self-enrichment
   psychology, split outward/physical vs inward/cerebral. Discriminator in
   ELICITATION-SPEC §8.4.

9. THE ORGANISER   [renamed from "The Planner" — dual-coded on purpose]
   Friend: books the trip, runs the group, the one who makes things happen.
   Character: WANTS to make things happen — to be the one who moves people and gets
   it done; NEEDS/PROTECTS their agency and the group that relies on it; LIE: "it's
   only logistics; I'm just the practical one." GHOST: a need to be useful and
   indispensable — and a real discomfort with things left undone or left to chance.
   Break-mode: watched; the highest-agency BURN risk. The mode is CASCADE-BY-ACTION —
   if things splinter, they're the one who turns latent discontent into coordinated
   movement, because organising is simply what they do with a problem. The reason it
   might NOT break: the Lie keeps them from seeing their own mobilisation capacity —
   they read themselves as "just practical," apolitical, so they never aim the
   capability they have.
   Peg: #1 COORDINATION — "organiser" is literally the flag (mobilises people).
   Join pull: Belonging + being-needed.
   Edge: the dual meaning IS the joke — event-planner AND political-organiser,
   same word. Carries the coordination slot. "Mobilisation capacity: present."
   Flags: pairs with Social Linchpin (#11) — OVERTLY makes things happen (the visible
   driver) vs QUIETLY bridges across the social graph (the connective thread). Acts-on-
   the-group vs is-the-bridge-between-groups; make the contrast explicit (it's a fun
   group-chat compare). Discriminator in ELICITATION-SPEC §8.4.

10. THE WILDCARD   [renamed from "Chaos Agent" 2026-06-12 — "Agent" implied
                    intent/villainy (the Enforcer trap); "Wildcard" keeps the
                    illegibility, drops the menace, more group-chat-native]
   Friend: unpredictable, no fixed signature, says wild things, can't be pinned.
   Character: WANTS not to be pinned — and, underneath, protects themselves by
   staying unplaceable; NEEDS/PROTECTS the real self the wildness keeps out of reach;
   LIE: "the unreadable surface IS me / it keeps the real me safe" — the chaos is a
   front, whether or not they know it's one. GHOST: a fear of being truly seen — that
   if anyone reached past the noise to the actual person, that's the exposure that
   frightens them.
   Break-mode: doesn't hinge on deliberate-vs-innate — performed chaos and native
   chaos land the same. The mode is SCRAMBLE-AS-SELF-PROTECTION: an individual seeding
   noise to stay unreachable. And the sting is that it doesn't work — the scramble is
   itself a consistent pattern, so they stay MAPPABLE; the front buys a slow read, not
   a missed one.
   Peg: #2/#3 — presents as anomaly/incoherence, but the incoherence resolves into a
   readable pattern on inspection (the unplaceability is itself the placement).
   Join pull: ambiguous.
   Edge: the IJOP anomaly read inverted — the wild surface looks unreadable and isn't.
   "Pattern: presents as none stable; resolves on observation." Carries the slow
   "unusual pattern / additional data required" reveal — but it lands on we-see-you-
   anyway: the front took longer, the system reached the person behind it regardless.
   Flags: the deliberate-or-genuine question doesn't change the routing (mappable
   either way) — "Wildcard" keeps the playful surface; the dossier sting is that the
   surface was a front and it was read through.

-- PRIORITY INTEREST tail (rare) --

11. SOCIAL LINCHPIN   [restored from parking lot, 2026-06-12]
   Friend: NOT the loudest or most prominent — the quiet connector who somehow knows
   people in every circle and keeps them all linked. The thread the whole network runs
   through: the one who reaches across groups that otherwise wouldn't touch, so without
   them the room quietly comes apart. (The anthropology read: cohesion lives not in the
   most visible person but in the one who bridges across the social graph.)
   Character: WANTS the people and groups they love to stay connected and whole — to be
   the thread between them, giving and receiving belonging; NEEDS/PROTECTS the bonds and
   the web of connection itself; LIE: "I'm just sociable; this is only my friends."
   GHOST: belonging is the whole point — being the thread is both how they're loved and
   how they love.
   Break-mode: watched hardest; highest LATENT Burn — though they'd never cast
   themselves that way. The mode is CASCADE-BY-CONDUIT: they don't act, but everything
   MOVES THROUGH them — and because they bridge otherwise-separate clusters, a cascade
   runs along their connections precisely where the network is most fragile; isolating
   them shatters it. The reason it stays latent: they'd never see themselves as a
   threat — they're "just" sociable, and the Lie hides the centrality entirely.
   Peg: #1 COORDINATION as the master objective in human form — high BETWEENNESS, the
   bridge across the social graph (the secondary-surveillance-network node). The
   quietest-seeming person is the most watched.
   Join pull: Belonging (gives and receives it most of anyone).
   Edge: the rare Priority-Interest hit — "betweenness centrality: high; bridges
   disconnected clusters; resistant to isolation vectors; primary cascade vector." The
   sting: you're THE one, and only because you quietly hold everyone together.
   Flags: the rare acute tail (balances the No-Concern tail). DISTINCT FROM Organiser
   on purpose, and the contrast is sharper now — the Organiser OVERTLY makes things
   happen (visible driver, high conscientiousness); the Linchpin QUIETLY bridges and
   connects across the graph (quieter than you'd expect for someone so connected; low
   neuroticism, high agreeableness). Acts-on-the-group vs is-the-bridge-between-groups.
   A fun and clean group-chat compare — spell it out in both dossiers (CANOE is only
   corroborating colour here; the split is the character). Discriminator in
