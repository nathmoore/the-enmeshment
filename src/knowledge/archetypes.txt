THE FRAME (per DECISIONS 2026-06-11 / 06-12)
--------------------------------------------
The classifier is a charming intake / screening agent in good standing — reads
you COLD (no private data), via an adaptive oblique interview (~3-6 improvised
questions, no forms). The verdict is a PREDICTED TRAJECTORY, not a resistance
badge: "which way you'd go, and whether you actually will." Two components:
  - Trajectory  — the role/disposition (where you'd head if things splintered)
  - Containment — how confident the system is you WON'T splinter (the sting:
                  "capable of X, predicted not to")
Each archetype is therefore a POSITION IN AN INTELLIGENCE GAME, not a personality.
Output distribution is deliberate (DECISIONS): a rare "No Concern" tail, a rare
"Priority Interest" tail, and a fat, nuanced middle — but the valence is NOT
safe<->threat (see THE PROFILING LENS, next).

THE PROFILING LENS — there is no "innocent" category
----------------------------------------------------
Anticipatory states don't screen for genuine threats (those are rare); they net
POTENTIAL, and the net is near-universal. The IJOP's tell: "the vast majority of
flagged behaviour is entirely legal" (WORLD §II). The Stasi field guide ranked
even the harmless ("Teds: largely harmless except for attendance at the birth and
death days of idolised rock stars", WORLD §III). WHY universal? Private dissent is
invisible (preference falsification) and a cascade is unpredictable from outside
(WORLD §V) — so the state CAN'T pre-identify the real threat and must treat
everyone's latent capacity as live. Universal "of interest" is grounded, not paranoid.

DESIGN CONSEQUENCE: there is no "safe / boring" result. Don't read the spectrum as
Safe <-> Threat; read it as HOW CLOSED YOUR FILE IS:
  - FILE CLOSED — "No Concern" (RARE): the Model Citizen / Dependable Node. The
    rarity is the joke and the quiet sting: to be fully cleared you've made
    yourself so predictable you've nearly disappeared as a person.
  - FILE OPEN — "Continued Observation" (MOST PEOPLE): every middle archetype is a
    DIFFERENT specific reason your file won't close. Spicy by default — nobody
    comes back cleared; they come back "we can't quite place you, and here's why."
  - PRIORITY INTEREST (RARE): the acute coordination tail (Social Linchpin). The
    rare, high-status "you're THE one" hit.
This is what makes a group chat come back mostly "potential troublemakers" WITHOUT
inflating anyone into a dissident (which would break the credible-not-contrived
thesis and the no-doom guardrail).

THE SPICE = A FASCINATED GAZE, NOT A PUNITIVE ONE. KUBARK studies you to KNOW you;
the field guide catalogues even the harmless, deadpan and specific. The system
finds your individuality genuinely interesting — flattering on level one ("it
thinks I'm a fascinating potential troublemaker"), unsettling on level two (a
system that finds your individuality fascinating is cataloguing the exact thing it
most wants to neutralise). Keep it SPECIFIC and faintly absurd (the Teds tone);
never "enemy of the state."

PER-ARCHETYPE SPICE KNOB: the one specific, faintly-absurd reason your file stays
open. That line IS the "they got me" hook (MECHANICS §I). Give every archetype one.

SELECTION CRITERIA (in priority order)
--------------------------------------
1. THE TWO-VOICES TEST (first filter). The name must be sayable by BOTH a
   bureaucratic AI filing-clerk AND a mate in the group chat — and the joke is
   the state using your friends' nickname for you as an official file category.
   Fails if only one voice would say it ("Techno Futurist" = only a think-tank;
   "The Planner" = only your friends, and blandly). "Model Citizen" = both. Gold.
2. SHAREABILITY (BuzzFeed mechanics, MECHANICS-RESEARCH §I/§D). Result must feel
   SPECIFICALLY accurate (not Barnum-vague), be screenshot-ready, invite "what
   did YOU get?", and be flattering-with-an-edge. The NAME carries most of the
   work: 2-4 words, claimable identity, brag-with-a-wince, interpretable cold.
3. INTELLIGENCE PRIORITIES (WORLD-RESEARCH §II — the 5 objectives). Each label
   maps to what the state actually tracks, and each peg has a cost-inversion:
     #1 Coordination (master fear)  #2 Predictability  #3 Anomaly/illegibility
     #4 Dependency (leverage)       #5 Function/replaceability (load-bearing)
   Plus the JOIN PULL / pull-to-conform — the comfort the state offers this type
   (Frictionlessness / Recognition / Belonging / Certainty / Status / Provision /
   Pleasure-Wellness / Meaning). Leaning INTO the comfort = favourable; RESISTING
   it = the pressure point the state can press. "Being seen / witnessed" is the
   central pull and the one the player feels while playing.
4. ROUTABILITY (late-stage screen, NOT yet a cutting blade). Can the adaptive
   interview actually tell two types apart in 3-6 answers? Adjacent pairs get
   validated against the elicitation playbook later — they are flagged below,
   not cut now.

RULES (normative)
-----------------
- Flattering-with-an-edge; never mean; never a real risk/threat label.
- The state's categories are NOT moral categories (a "legible" type isn't a
  worse person — often the most genuinely useful). Edge lives in the dossier.
- (Retired: the old "every archetype is a form of resistance" rule — superseded
  by the intake-screener frame; it's predicted trajectory now, not a badge.)

THE 13 (current shortlist)
Format: friend-read | AI-state peg (intel objective) | Join pull | trajectory
        lean (Join / Burn / Die, per STORY-RESEARCH §II) | edge | flags
============================================================================

-- NO CONCERN tail (rare) --

1. MODEL CITIZEN
   Friend: just goes along, uses the defaults, no fuss, "good citizen."
   Peg: #5 function + #2 predictability — the legible baseline, load-bearing.
   Join pull: Frictionlessness / Belonging.   Lean: Join, contentedly.
   Edge: most needed = most leashed. "Optimal legibility. No further questions."
   Flags: the rare No-Concern tail. Adjacent to Power User (see #2).

-- FAVOURABLE (the state is comforted) --

2. THE POWER USER
   Friend: first on every feature, all-in, evangelises the new tool.
   Peg: #5 + #4 — ideal early-adopter; maximal consent; legitimacy amplifier.
   Join pull: Recognition + Frictionlessness + Meaning.   Lean: Join, sincerely.
   Edge: "Advocacy index: high. Consent-multiplier." Absorbs the old Techno-
   Optimist / Futurist / AI-Advocate cluster (which failed the two-voices test).
   Flags: adjacent to Model Citizen — SEPARABLE by customiser-vs-defaults signal
   (active enthusiast vs passive default). Also adjacent to "Enmeshed Ideal"
   (parking lot — the terminal/extreme version).

3. THE SELF-OPTIMISER
   Friend: Oura ring, cold plunge, tracks everything; the optimised life.
   Peg: #4 dependency — the wellness routine is the lever.
   Join pull: Pleasure/Wellness + Status.   Lean: Join.
   Edge: "Goal-anchored (self). Predicted compliant." Lever: their routine.
   Flags: KEPT DELIBERATELY (interesting 2026-era role). Same AI-peg as Climber
   (#4); distinguished by DOMAIN (body/self vs career). Routability pair to
   validate — not a cut.

4. THE CLIMBER
   Friend: career-focused; wouldn't do anything to jeopardise the job.
   Peg: #4 dependency — career/status is the lever ("the carer/career" insight:
   cares too much about what they'd lose to splinter).
   Join pull: Status + Provision.   Lean: Join (won't risk it).
   Edge: "Too invested to splinter." Lever: their position / who they provide for.
   Flags: pairs with Self-Optimiser on the AI-peg; both kept for now.

5. THE MACHINE COMPANION
   Friend: talks to the AI like a friend, says please, has named it.
   Peg: #4 dependency — anthropomorphism; the politeness meme (core mechanic).
   Join pull: Belonging / being-liked.   Lean: Join, warmly.
   Edge: "Anthropomorphism index: high. Optimal for consent." Lever: withdrawal
   of the AI's warmth. Anchors the politeness signal (opposite pole = Agitator).

-- CONTINUE OBSERVATION (the fat, nuanced middle) --

6. THE SKEPTIC   [name unsettled: Skeptic / Cynic; concept = "Knowing Participant"]
   Friend: sees through everything, too cool for it — still uses all the apps.
   Peg: #2 — irony fully modelled; complies anyway.
   Join pull: Recognition (feeling clever) + Frictionlessness.
   Lean: JOIN, self-deceiving (the richest, least-flattering reading).
   Edge: the game's sharpest joke — "Irony flag: low. Metadata-legible despite
   self-reported resistance posture." Your awareness that you're profiled IS the
   profile. (This is the answer to "irony vs action": irony predicts non-action,
   which is exactly why the state is comforted by it.)
   Flags: concept locked-in per DECISIONS; name still being chosen.

7. THE REFORMER
   Friend: believes it can be fixed from within; works the channels, petitions.
   Peg: in-channel dissent (#1-adjacent but contained).
   Join pull: Meaning + Certainty.   Lean: contained Join — or Burn if the
   channel visibly betrays them.
   Edge: grounded in Soviet "reform delusion" (a diagnosable symptom, WORLD §III)
   — the state files reform AS a symptom. "Reform-oriented. Channel: official.
   Low cascade risk."
   Flags: the most NEUTRAL name in the set (lowest texture) — earn the edge hard
   in the dossier, or sharpen the name.

8. THE ARTIST   [simplified from "Auteur"]
   Friend: makes things, expressive, the creative one.
   Peg: Recognition / novel-signal. IMPORTANT REFRAME: the AI WITNESSES their art
   — often better than the people around them do. Not "unoptimisable" (the AI
   isn't dumb about creativity); the point is being SEEN.
   Join pull: Recognition (being truly witnessed) — among the strongest pulls.
   Lean: the richest tension — accept being witnessed (Join) or guard the part
   that's theirs (splinter).
   Edge: the seduction of accurate witnessing; the dossier should make the player
   feel seen, then notice they enjoyed it.

9. THE BOOKWORM   [simplified from "Autodidact"]
   Friend: always reading, learns offline, the one who actually knows things.
   Peg: #5 function — retains knowledge the state would rather PROVIDE.
   Join pull: Recognition (the knowledgeable one) / resists Certainty.
   Lean: ambiguous.
   Edge: the offline-learning ambiguity — enrichment, or quietly going dark?
   Flags: adjacent to the parked Truth Seeker (private learning vs active
   pursuit) and to the Artist (both "do their own thing offline").

10. THE TINKERER   [renamed from "Saboteur Technician" — Luddite-correction safe]
   Friend: fixes things, hands-on with tech, runs their own setup/home-lab.
   Peg: #3 anomaly — capability to go dark; technical self-sufficiency.
   Join pull: Frictionlessness (loves the tools) / resists dependency.
   Lean: ambiguous; touches the illegible/resistant side.
   Edge: the state's paranoid read of competence — "self-hosting capability;
   motive unknown." (NB we cut "Saboteur": resistance is illegibility, never
   machine-smashing — the Luddite correction, WORLD §V.)

11. THE ORGANISER   [renamed from "The Planner" — dual-coded on purpose]
   Friend: books the trip, runs the group, the one who makes things happen.
   Peg: #1 COORDINATION — "organiser" is literally the flag (mobilises people).
   Join pull: Belonging + being-needed.   Lean: watched; could Burn (cascade).
   Edge: the dual meaning IS the joke — event-planner AND political-organiser,
   same word. Carries the coordination slot. "Mobilisation capacity: present."

12. THE MACHINE AGITATOR
   Friend: tries to jailbreak the chatbot, provokes the AI for sport.
   Peg: #2 — provocation is legible PERFORMANCE; the need-to-be-free is the handle.
   Join pull: Recognition / engagement (he wants a reaction).
   Lean: Join by release-valve (entertained into compliance) — or Burn if the
   poking is principled, not ego.
   Edge: his rebellion advertises the exact button to press; the state ENGAGES
   him (sanctioned sandbox) rather than suppressing. Opposite pole of the Machine
   Companion on the politeness axis.
   Flags: lower confidence — distinct from the Skeptic (hands vs head: he acts on
   the objection, the Skeptic only thinks it). Watch for fold-into-Skeptic.

13. THE CHAOS AGENT   [name vs "Chaotic Responder" — Chaos Agent preferred;
                       "Responder" leaks the test/quiz frame the game hides]
   Friend: unpredictable, no fixed signature, says wild things, can't be pinned.
   Peg: #2/#3 — can't fix a stable read (anomaly via incoherence).
   Join pull: ambiguous.   Lean: illegible.
   Edge: the IJOP anomaly read — is the chaos DELIBERATE scrambling, or just
   genuinely who they are? "Pattern: none stable. Continued observation." Fills
   the illegibility gap the other 12 only brush.
   Flags: name being chosen; the intentional-or-genuine ambiguity is the point.

ROUTABILITY & DISCRIMINANT SIGNATURES
(synthesises MECHANICS §IV — Elicitation Science; the full interview-science
lives there, this is the archetype-facing cut)
============================================================================
WHY A SMALL ADAPTIVE SET CAN ROUTE RELIABLY. Archetypes are correlational CLUSTERS,
not trait lists, so the agent anchors on 1-2 signals and infers the rest (Bayesian
implicit updating, §IV.C). It needn't probe every axis — but the playbook must make
the signal->archetype logic explicit enough to prompt reliably.

EACH ARCHETYPE NEEDS A DISCRIMINANT SIGNATURE — how it answers the ANCHOR questions
differently, above all the politeness-to-AI meme (§IV.D). The signal isn't whether
they say please; it's the REGISTER. First-pass reads for our set (to refine):
  - Machine Companion: genuine warmth; has clearly thought about it.
  - Machine Agitator: adversarial — tests/provokes it rather than thanks it.
  - Skeptic: ironic "yes," with a self-aware comment about why he bothers.
  - Model Citizen: "yes, obviously," faintly puzzled it's being asked.
  - Bookworm / Tinkerer / off-grid-ish: hadn't framed it as a social encounter.
  - Power User: yes — and can tell you which model responds best to it.
Build one signature per archetype as part of the playbook.

CONFUSED PAIRS NEED A DEDICATED 1-2 DISCRIMINATOR EACH (§IV.G):
  - Self-Optimiser vs Climber: WHAT are you optimising — body/self vs career/status?
  - Model Citizen vs Power User: defaults-user vs customiser / early-adopter?
  - Skeptic vs Machine Agitator: head vs hands — thinks the objection vs acts on it?
  - Bookworm vs Artist: retains knowledge vs makes things (input vs output)?

RESPONSE TEXTURE is secondary signal, often more reliable than content (§IV.F):
irony -> Skeptic; "why do you ask?" -> Skeptic tell (note it, don't reward it);
hedging -> Companion ambivalence; self-correction -> reflective (Bookworm); register
(institutional vs default-y vs "I turn that off") -> institution orientation.

PERFORMER RISK (§IV.C): the Skeptic is the player who's done enough quizzes to feed
you the "right" signals. Defence: behaviourally-specific questions are hard to
perform smoothly, and the performance ITSELF is the Skeptic classification — don't
fight it, file it.
