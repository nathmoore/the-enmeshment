## Why this doc exists

The intake-agent reframe (DECISIONS 2026-06-11) superseded the old "defected
profiler" lore. Before we can lock the Name, the Output/dossier framing, or the
agent's voice, we need to see the whole world working together as one coherent,
specific picture — and stress it for contradictions. This is that workspace.

The discipline: go specific, weave it concretely, then look at the whole and ask
"does this hold?" What survives gets promoted to LORE.md (canon). What doesn't is
just a discarded draft — no loss, that's the point of a sandbox.
