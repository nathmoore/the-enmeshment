## Subject profile

The Skeptic notices things. The fine print, the detail everyone else skims, the update that doesn't quite add up — they catch it. Sharp, curious, genuinely hard to sell to. They question everything, especially anything official or automated, and they're usually right to. While the rest of us scroll, the Skeptic is the one actually reading.

## Assessment

Those critical-thinking skills would be a real asset to any resistance — were they ever to sign up. Reading the room, pressure-testing the plan, spotting the trap before anyone walks in. The catch: far likelier to see a movement coming than to be the spark that starts one — never quite the type to turn private doubt into a crowd. A valued strategic mind; for now, a very reliable AI subscriber.
