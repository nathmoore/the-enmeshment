## Subject profile

The Self-Optimiser is working on it — whatever "it" is this quarter. Sleep, steps, savings, the five-year plan: tracked, measured, trending the right way. Disciplined, driven, the one who actually sticks to the routine. If there's a better version of themselves going, they're already chasing it.

## Assessment

A resistance could use that discipline — nobody plans a contingency like someone who colour-codes their week. But there's too much plugged into the current setup to risk pulling the cord; the optimised life only runs while the system behind it does. The routine they're so proud of is, from where we sit, a leash they tightened themselves. On track. Predicted compliant.

## Field notes

- Owns the ring, the app, and the spreadsheet.
- Optimising at least one thing at all times.
- Goal-anchored. Unlikely to deviate.
