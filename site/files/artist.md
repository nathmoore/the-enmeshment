## Subject profile

The Artist makes things. Sees the angle no one else clocked, has a taste entirely their own, guards a piece of work that's theirs and no one else's. Expressive, original, a little protective of the good stuff. The friend whose recommendations you trust and whose things you'd actually turn up to see.

## Assessment

A resistance would love them — every movement needs someone who can make people feel something. The subtler catch is what they want most: to be seen. And the machine now speaks their language fluently enough to hand them that audience, if they'll just soften the edges. Conform a little, and the attention turns their way. Being understood has never been so available, or cost quite so much.
