## Subject profile

The Model Citizen keeps things running. No drama, no fuss, no strong opinions aired at dinner — just gets on with it. Uses the defaults, takes the sensible path, and quietly wonders why everyone else makes life so complicated. Reliable, easygoing, the one who never causes a scene.

The only catch is the quiet one underneath: to be read this easily, a person has to leave almost nothing of themselves sticking out. The cleanest file we hold. File status: No Concern.
