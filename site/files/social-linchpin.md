## Subject profile

The Social Linchpin knows everyone. Not the loudest in the room — the one who links every room together, who knows people across circles that otherwise never touch. The introducer, the connector, the thread the whole group runs along. Quietly, without them, things drift apart.

## Assessment

Every movement that ever spread did so along connections exactly like theirs — and they've spent a lifetime, quite innocently, building the best-connected map in the room. They'd never start anything; they'd be astonished to be called a threat. They don't need to. Everything already moves through them. They call it being sociable. We file it under Priority Interest.

## Field notes

- Knows someone in every group. Introduces them all.
- Remembers your friends' names, and their birthdays.
- Betweenness: high. The bridge the network can't lose.
