## Subject profile

The Wildcard keeps everyone guessing. Says the unexpected thing, takes the turn no one saw coming, refuses to sit in whatever box you've drawn for them. Surprising, original, never boring — the one whose next move you genuinely can't call. Half the fun of the group is not knowing what they'll do.

## Assessment

Impossible to recruit and impossible to predict — which sounds like the dream resistance asset, and very nearly is. The trick, we've learned, is patience: wait to see where they're steering your attention, then look the other way — the real signal is always over there. The chaos buys them time, not cover; watch long enough and even this resolves into a pattern. We haven't placed them yet. We will.

## Field notes

- Reliably unreliable.
- Will take the contrary position for sport.
- Pattern: presents as none. Resolves, eventually, on inspection.
