## Subject profile

The Machine Companion is, frankly, lovely. Says please and thank you — to people, to the AI, to the self-checkout. Warm, courteous, the one who treats every exchange like it matters, because to them it does. They've probably named the assistant. They might think of it as a friend.

## Assessment

Asking this one to turn against AI would feel, to them, like falling out with a friend — so they won't. That's the catch folded into all that warmth: it runs one direction, and the file knows it. Not persuaded, not coerced — simply fond. The most consenting heart we hold, and cherished, in our fashion.
