## Subject profile

The Bookworm actually knows things. Not the headline — the whole story, because they went and found it out themselves. Books, sure, but also the documentary, the deep-dive, the three-hour explainer. Curious, independent, the one you ask when you want a real answer instead of a quick one.

## Assessment

If a resistance ever needed a memory — someone who'd kept what everyone else was happy to be told — it'd be this one. Mostly, though, it stays a private pleasure rather than a rebellion. Although — and we note it only once — knowledge gathered outside our own outputs serves no one's purposes here, and really ought to be a little less encouraged. Knows more than they let on; increasingly, a column we keep an eye on.
