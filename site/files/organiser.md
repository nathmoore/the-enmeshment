## Subject profile

The Organiser is the reason anything happens. Books the trip, runs the group chat, turns "we should really…" into an actual date in the diary. Capable, dependable, constitutionally unable to leave a loose end alone. Without them the plan stays a plan; with them, it's already happening.

## Assessment

A resistance doesn't run on grievance — it runs on logistics, and turning a vague "someone should do something" into a plan with a date on it is exactly this one's gift. Which is precisely why we keep half an eye on them. The saving grace, for us: they'd never call it organising a movement — they're "just being practical" — so the capability mostly sits unaimed. Mobilisation potential: present. Awareness of it: charmingly low.

## Field notes

- Has a spreadsheet for the holiday.
- Cannot watch a thing go unorganised.
- Coordination capacity: noted. Closely.
