## Subject profile

The Power User got there first. First to the new feature, the new app, the update the rest of us are still squinting at — fluent in tools nobody's finished reading the manual for. They don't just use the thing, they evangelise it, and they're genuinely good at it. The one the group asks when something's confusing.

## Assessment

If they joined a resistance they'd turn up with the best kit and the manual already read — but that's the thing, they won't. Far too invested in the future arriving on schedule. Every tool they master, they master out loud, bringing everyone along. Not just sold: selling. Our most valuable kind of user, and we're grateful.

## Field notes

- On the waitlist for things that don't have a waitlist yet.
- Strong opinions on which model is best.
- Adoption rate: exemplary.
