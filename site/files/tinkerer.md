## Subject profile

The Tinkerer always has a project on the go. Fixes the thing rather than binning it, makes what they can't buy, happiest with their hands busy and something half-apart on the table. Practical, self-reliant, faintly allergic to doing it the official way. The friend you call when something breaks.

## Assessment

A resistance would want them for the obvious reason — they can actually make and mend, off the grid and off the record. And that, more than any politics, is what gives us pause: a person who can do for themselves is a person who needs us less, and shows us less. No manifesto, no grand refusal — just capable hands and a quiet, low-yield self-sufficiency. Motive: unfiled.

## Field notes

- Owns the tool for the one job. And the spares.
- Distrusts anything that can't be opened and fixed.
- Self-sufficient to a degree we can't quite account for.
